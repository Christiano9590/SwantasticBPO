About Us

About the Department

The Payroll Department of Swantastic Business Process Outsourcing (Swantastic BPO) is responsible for the preparation of employee salaries, ensuring that salaries are calculated accurately and on time. The department manages employee salary records, overtime, taxes, and deductions while maintaining confidentiality and following company policies.

Our Core Values

  • Accuracy in every calculation
  • Timely processing & distribution
  • Strict confidentiality
  • Policy compliance
  • Clear communication
